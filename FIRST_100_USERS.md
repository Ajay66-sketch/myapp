# The First 100 Users: Cold-Start Growth Framework

To secure our first 100 active, highly retained users, we must ignore paid ads and focus on manual, highly focused distribution channels where frustrated students gather.

---

## 📈 Phase 1: The Reddit Vulnerability Playbook

Students on Reddit are highly hostile to direct sales pitches, but highly receptive to student-builders solving their own problems.

### Target Subreddits:
- **`r/getstudying`** (400k+ members)
- **`r/productivity`** (2M+ members)
- **`r/ADHD`** (1.5M+ members - focus heavily on ADHD body-doubling)
- **`r/studytips`** (120k+ members)

### Post Formula (The Vulnerable Indie Hacker):
1. **The Hook**: "I was failing my physics classes because I couldn't focus for more than 10 minutes at a time without checking social media."
2. **The Problem**: Explaining how existing video study streams are too distracting and ChatGPT feels isolated.
3. **The Solution**: "So I spent my semester break building a text-only, gamified synchronized study dashboard with a built-in AI textbook coach. No cameras, no social anxiety, just a synchronized timer and streak freezes to save focus streaks."
4. **The CTA (Call to Action)**: "It's 100% free while I'm in beta. I just need 50 students to test it with me and tell me where it breaks. Drop a comment and I'll send you an invite link."

---

## 💬 Phase 2: Discord Study Circles Integration

We will not build a Discord server from scratch (too much friction). Instead, we will piggyback on existing academic servers:

1. **Target Mid-Tier Servers**: Find student-run AP prep, MCAT prep, or university-specific Discord servers (500–5,000 members).
2. **Identify Moderation Partners**: Contact the student mods. Offer their community an exclusive **Co-Branded Study Corridor** in our platform (e.g. "r/APStudents Official Focus Corridor").
3. **Reciprocal Rewards**: Provide their top active students with free lifetime Pro elite upgrades in exchange for pinning the corridor link in their study chat channels.

---

## 🎥 Phase 3: TikTok & YouTube shorts Loop

Short-form content must be built around visual "aesthetic studying" trends:

- **The Aesthetic**: Standard low-light, warm desk setup with lofi beats playing.
- **The Hook**: "Websites that feel illegal for students with ADHD."
- **The Video Loop**:
  - Show a phone screen loading a social feed -> screen transitions to our dark glassmorphic dashboard (`Circular Pomodoro Timer` counting down).
  - Zoom in on the active countdown and shows the user typing notes in the PDF scanner.
  - Show the text-chat corridor message: *"Keep going! Only 5 minutes left in this Pomodoro!"*
- **Call-to-Action**: "Link in bio to join active corridors."

---

## 🔄 Phase 4: Streak-Loss Referral Triggers

Leverage loss-aversion psychology to build a growth loop directly in our user interface:

1. **The Trigger Event**: A student misses their daily study target, putting their 15-day study streak at risk of decay.
2. **The Resolution Offer**:
   ```
   🚨 STREAK DECAY WARNING!
   Your 15-day streak is about to freeze or reset.
   
   Get 1 Free STREAK FREEZE immediately by sharing your referral code 
   with 1 classmate. If they sign up, your streak is preserved!
   
   [Share Invite Code via WhatsApp / Discord]
   ```
3. **Outcome**: Highly viral distribution when students are most incentivized to share.
