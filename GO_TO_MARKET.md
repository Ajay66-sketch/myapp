# Go-To-Market & Revenue Acceleration Blueprint

This guide presents our formal launch phases, organic conversion flywheels, pricing validations, and SaaS cohort metrics targets to scale the Scholar platform from zero to $5K monthly recurring revenue (MRR).

---

## 📅 1. Go-To-Market Launch Phases

```
┌──────────────────────────┐      ┌──────────────────────────┐      ┌──────────────────────────┐
│   Phase 1: Alpha Sandbox  │ ───> │  Phase 2: Organic Invite  │ ───> │  Phase 3: Public Launch  │
│      Weeks 1 to 4        │      │      Weeks 5 to 8        │      │      Week 9 onwards      │
│  - 50 student beta testers│      │  - Peer referral loops   │      │  - Direct pricing gates  │
│  - Eliminate timer bugs  │      │  - Custom campus corridors│      │  - Campus newsletter ads │
└──────────────────────────┘      └──────────────────────────┘      └──────────────────────────┘
```

### Phase 1: The High-Accountability Beta (Weeks 1-4)
- **Goal**: Secure 50 core students who study at least 3 times a week.
- **Action**: Manually invite students from r/getstudying and r/studytips. Keep standard subscriptions free, but collect deep feedback on timer bugs and AI prompt quality.

### Phase 2: Campus Ambassadors Program (Weeks 5-8)
- **Goal**: Scale from 100 to 1,000 active users.
- **Action**: Partner with university study circles. Appoint 5 "Campus Ambassadors" and unlock free lifetime Pro upgrades for them if they invite 20 active classmates from their department.

### Phase 3: The Public Monetization Gate (Week 9+)
- **Goal**: Convert 5% of active free users to paying Pro subscribers.
- **Action**: Activate hourly limits on standard AI tutor usage and restrict streak freezes. Direct standard users to the upgrade pricing matrices.

## 📈 2. Core SaaS Metrics & Financial Health Rules

To maintain high capital efficiency, GTM campaigns must target these SRE-style business health metrics:

- **Week-4 Cohort Retention (Target > 30%)**: If W4 retention drops below 25%, halt all acquisition spend immediately. Product-market fit is not established, and more marketing will only lead to high churn.
- **LTV / CAC Ratio (Target > 3x)**:
  - Projected Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) must be 3 times greater than Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC).
  - Since students have a high churn propensity, we must target **organic, free acquisition channels** to keep CAC close to $0.
- **AI Gross Margins (Target > 85%)**: Audit `AdminRevenueDashboard` regularly to confirm token usage costs never exceed 15% of active monthly subscription MRR.

## 🚀 3. Organic Search Engine Optimization (SEO) Anchors

To secure long-term, high-intent traffic without ongoing advertising spend, we will deploy organic keyword landing anchors:

1. **Focus on High-Intent Keywords**:
   - *"ADHD study timer synchronized"*
   - *"Online study group no camera"*
   - *"Virtual study room lofi"*
2. **Landing Page Strategy**: Create lightweight SEO landing pages optimized for search engines (e.g. `scholar.antigravity.io/tools/adhd-timer`), embedding our `Circular Pomodoro Timer` widget directly. This provides immediate utility and captures organic search traffic.
3. **Productivity Resource Hub**: Publish study guides and notes-taking tips that double as tutorials for using our AI text summarizer, linking back to our login screen.
